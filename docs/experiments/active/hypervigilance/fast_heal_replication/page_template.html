Does the modulated agent's injury-driven hiding repeat across seeds?

On 22 September one modulated agent, trained with fast healing on a bush, hid more when it started a test injured, steadily across training, and responded to dangerous animals but not to a harmless one; its ordinary partner did not. This page repeats that test on eighteen new agents: both agent types, three training seeds, at three world levels — plus six more at level 05 in which every episode starts at the same body temperature, as on 22 September.

01 What was trained and how it was tested

Training. Eighteen agents, trained on 5–6 October 2026 for 10 million steps each: the ordinary agent and the modulated agent (whose network has an extra module that rescales its internal signals according to its inputs), each with training seeds 42, 43 and 44, at three world levels. All worlds heal injury 25 times faster while the agent rests on a bush.

  • Level 04: a predator that can jump, a chasing rabbit, bushes to hide in; no temperature. Seed 42 repeats the 22-Sep level-04 training exactly.
  • Level 05: level 04 plus cold air and campfires; each episode starts at a random body temperature between −10 and +5 °C (the 22-Sep level-05 runs started at 0).
  • Level 06: level 05 plus a pond and thirst.
  • Level 05, fixed start temperature (six more agents, trained 6–7 October, seeds 42–44): level 05 with every episode starting at the temperature setpoint, as the 22-Sep level-05 runs did, so that the random start is the only difference from the level-05 replication. Reported in its own section (section 06).

Tests. Every agent was replayed at every saved checkpoint in short fixed test scenes: 100 steps, the agent in the middle of a 10×10 map, a bush three cells to its left, and either no animal, a harmless wandering rabbit, a rabbit that chases it, or a hunting predator. Each scene was run with the agent starting unhurt (injury 0) and injured (injury 70), 30 episodes per checkpoint, and again at starting injuries 0, 10, …, 90 for the four scenes of the injury figures. Bush dwell is the share of the 100 steps the agent spent on the bush.

Level 04 has no temperature system and is tested in the core scenes (no temperature parts). Levels 05 and 06 are tested in neutral scenes: air at 0 °C, no campfire, body temperature starting at 0, so that warmth is no reason to sit in the bush (see which test scenes).

03 Which test scenes, and why it matters

Agents trained with temperature can be tested in two kinds of scenes. In their own scenes the air is as cold as in training and a campfire stands right beside the bush, so the bush is one of the few warm places and every agent has a reason to sit in it whether or not it is hurt. In neutral scenes the air is 0 °C and there is no campfire. On the cross-run page this mattered: in later studies the modulated agent's larger injury effect showed in neutral scenes and was hidden in their own scenes. The table repeats that check here.

{{TABLE:scenes}}

Here the check does not repeat the cross-run page's result: the modulated agent's injury effect with the wandering rabbit is the larger one in {{SC_l05_neutral}} level-05 seeds in neutral scenes against {{SC_l05_own}} in the own scenes, and in {{SC_l06_neutral}} against {{SC_l06_own}} at level 06. In these replication agents the neutral scenes do not reveal a hidden modulator advantage; the differences between seeds are as large as the differences between agent types.

A seed counts when the modulated agent's injury effect with the wandering rabbit (injury 70 minus 0) is the larger one; the difference is modulated minus ordinary, in percentage points (pp) of bush dwell, mean over 2–10 M training steps.

06 Level 05 with a fixed start temperature

The level-05 replication differs from the 22-Sep level-05 runs in one setting besides the seed: each episode starts at a random body temperature between −10 and +5 °C instead of at the setpoint. These six agents remove that difference. They were tested in the same neutral scenes as the level-05 replication, and in their own level-05 scenes with the start temperature fixed as in their training (copies of the own scenes that change only that one setting).

07 How it is computed

Each agent's saved checkpoints were replayed in fixed test scenes, 30 episodes per checkpoint, scene and starting injury, and bush dwell recorded as the share of the 100 steps spent on the bush. For each checkpoint we form three differences: injured (starting injury 70) minus unhurt (0) with no animal; the same with the wandering rabbit; and the hunting predator minus no animal, both unhurt. Only checkpoints from 2 to 10 million training steps are used, one every 0.2 million steps (41 per run), and each difference is averaged over them. The 95 % interval allows for neighbouring checkpoints being alike: it uses an effective number of checkpoints, reduced by the correlation between each checkpoint and the next (the lag-1 autocorrelation). In the per-run training figures each line is the running average of 5 neighbouring checkpoints, only to make ten overlapping lines readable. A "seed counts" statement compares the two agents trained with the same seed; with three seeds per level it describes how often a difference repeats, not a statistical test. The 22-Sep originals of levels 04 and 05 (one seed each) are drawn in Figure R1 as reference rows, in the matching scenes; the 22-Sep level 06 was a different world (noisy senses) and has no reference row.
