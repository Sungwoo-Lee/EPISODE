The clearest change is not in injury-driven hiding. Memory 32 with groups of 32 hides much more in every scene between about 2 and 6 M steps, even unhurt and with no animal (Figure S11). Its predator effect (+19 pp, against +36 to +57 pp for the references) is small because the no-animal baseline is high, not because it ignores the predator. In the input screen, the two settings that read felt injury but not the outside world hide more with the wandering rabbit at every starting injury (Figure S8), which raises the level more than the slope.
