In all eight worlds the ideal planner's best move is to hide in a bush in {{hide_min}}–{{hide_max}} % of start states and to go out to eat in only {{eat_min}}–{{eat_max}} %; the bar needed both at 20 % (for eating, about {{bar_hungry}} % of the hungry start states). The closest is world 4 — the new predator setting with fewer, bigger food items — at {{w4_eat}} %. Only {{nt_min}}–{{nt_max}} % of start states have hiding and eating within 1 reward unit of each other.

When the agent is fed, injury above about 20 sends it to a bush; when hungry, it tolerates more injury before hiding. This is consistent with fast bush healing plus the bush being the only hazard-free place: healing first and eating afterwards costs little. Fewer, bigger food items move the balance most toward eating (+{{eF_B_abs}} points); invisible hazards move it toward hiding.
