# Review: "Water at Level 06" design page (analysis-verdict gate)

> **Object reviewed**: `docs/develop/active/thirst/thirst_water.template.html` (built page `thirst_water.html`), its simulator `scripts/analysis/studies/thirst_water/watersim.py`, the sweep `results/analysis/thirst_water/sweep.json`, and figure scripts w1–w9.
> **Supports**: [[THIRST_WATER_PLAN]] — settled decisions were not reopened.
> **Reviewed by**: plan-reviewer, 2026-09-29.

## Verdict

**SUPPORTED WITH CAVEATS.** The page's central claim — that at the planned settings (a 10×10 grid, thirst killing 160 steps after the comfortable middle, the pond at one of four corner spots) water is a gentle need, costing a scripted agent about 3 percentage points of full-length episodes (95 % against 98 % with water switched off) — is supported, and I checked that it does not move when the scripted agent's own choices (how late it goes for water, how full it drinks) are varied. The correction to the plan is also right: the plan's "about 5 % of episodes are dead of thirst by step 16 whatever the agent does" is a ceiling, and an agent that heads for the pond loses 1.8 % (knowing where the pond is) to 2.4 % (having to find it).

One headline number is **not** a property of the rules. The page says that at 20×20 "an agent that must find the pond survives in only 41 % of episodes". That figure belongs to the scripted agent's timing rule: it only starts looking for the pond when 25 steps of spare time remain, and treats the nearest candidate spot as if it were the pond. Give the same agent a 50-step margin and 20×20 survival is 66 %; give it 100 and it is 77 %. The qualitative message — grid size is the strongest of the dials tested — survives at every margin, but the sentence "finding the pond is where it bites" and the numbers in Figures 5, 6 and the lever table in section 14 are one-third policy. That is the Critical finding, and it is a wording-and-labelling fix, not a rerun.

A second, smaller policy dependence: "at gain 20.6, 10.8 % of episodes die of over-drinking" falls to 3.2 % if the scripted agent stops drinking at 120 instead of 160. The mechanism the page describes is right; the magnitude is the agent's.

## Simulator checks (no number-moving bug found)

Read `watersim.py` end to end and ran it. Death-label priority matches the plan's call 7; fires cannot land on the pond; food and the agent start off pond and fire; the cold-clock lookup is monotone over the start range; the search bookkeeping rules out a candidate only after one of its cells is seen. Two definitional choices move reported numbers without being bugs — the visit count includes walk-throughs (finding 6) and the walk back from the pond is labelled as fire-ring time (finding 3); both are quantified above. Caption numbers were spot-checked against `sweep.json` (Figures 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 all match to the rounding shown).

## Prior art

`docs/develop/active/issues/KNOWN_BUGS.md` has no row for water, thirst, hydration or pond; the only thirst document is the plan itself. Nothing to hand to `bug-curator`.

## Cost of being wrong

If the 20×20 must-find figure is read as a property of the rules, the "whole-ladder" grid-size decision the page feeds in section 14 is made on a number that is one-third the scripted agent's timing rule — a wrong design choice, but a cheap one to reverse before any code or training exists. Everything else on the page is wording; nothing here risks data or a training run.

## Exit condition for the Critical finding

Either add the margin band (25 / 50 / 100) to §01, Figure 5, Figure 6 and §14 with the wording above, or re-run the two `*_search` sweeps with an agent that starts looking early and replace the numbers. Owner: the page's author (the simulation session); no code under `src/` is involved.
