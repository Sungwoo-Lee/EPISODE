# Review: the B2 "wake-up timing" reading and the interpretation drafted for the page

Reviewed by: plan-reviewer · 2026-09-30 · analysis-verdict gate (mode b), before publication

## Verdict

**NOT SUPPORTED BY THE EVIDENCE SHOWN** — for the two interpretive sentences drafted for the page. The per-measure readings themselves (the table of "late" / "early" / "undetermined" per measure, produced by the pre-registered rule) are correctly computed and may go on the page verbatim. What the evidence does not support is what the draft says they *mean*.

Plain-language summary. The analysis asks, for each of seventeen ways of measuring the modulator's activity, whether that measure makes half of its total change *before* or *after* the agent's survival has levelled off. The draft interpretation says: the modulator learns *what* to respond to early (its output becomes context-dependent before survival plateaus), but its *authority* over the network (the gain swing) only grows afterwards, so it "wakes up late"; and freezing it costs nothing measurable at any point. Three of those claims are contradicted by the same output file the readings come from:

1. **The context-dependent share of the modulator's output does not form early — it *shrinks* early.** Every one of the 16 worlds records the contextual fraction (rho) as *falling* from its untrained value (about 0.18–0.23 at the random network) to about 0.07–0.18 at the end. "Early" here means half of that *decline* is complete by the first few checkpoints. The draft has the direction backwards.
2. **"The gain swing grows late" is what any weight norm does in this trainer.** The gain swing is not a measured activity; it is a weights-only ceiling — twice the average absolute-weight sum of the modulator's head matrix. It grows almost linearly across all of training, so its half-way point lands near mid-training by construction, while survival plateaus in the first third. Applying the identical registered rule to the main network's own total weight norm gives "late" in 16 of 16 worlds too (the network that has already learned the task has covered only a third of its own weight growth at the plateau). The reading is real; the inference "authority grows after the task is learned" is not.
3. **"Freezing costs nothing measurable" is not what "undetermined" means, and the curves say the opposite.** The rule asks whether the freeze cost *changes over training* by more than its checkpoint-to-checkpoint wobble; it does not (wobble 11–28 survival steps), so no *timing* is named. The *level* of the cost after the plateau is negative in 16 of 16 worlds: on average −33 survival steps for the frozen gain and −31 for the frozen offset, against a live survival of about 220–290 steps (roughly a 12 % loss).

The five swing readings are one fact counted five times (the five per-site curves correlate at 0.76–0.99 within a run), and the "early" reading on the modulator's relative update size is a reading on a curve that moves by about 10 % over all of training.

## Assumptions the interpretation rests on

- ❓ **rho falling = "less context dependence".** rho = across-time variance ÷ total variance, where total also includes the across-unit spread of the per-unit *means* (the constant, gauge part). rho can fall because the constant part spreads out across units even if the contextual variance is unchanged. Not checked by anyone; `var_across_time` is stored per point and should be shown. Owner: `experiment-analyzer` / `developer`.
- ❓ **The sign test's null (late and early equally likely) fits every measure.** For a monotone unbounded quantity the null is not ½; the registered test cannot distinguish "the modulator's ceiling grows after learning" from "weight norms grow linearly". Registered and unmovable (`revision_policy`); the page must carry the control (C2) beside the reading.
- Verified: the untrained anchor is the trainer's own construction (Checkpoint 4.4: bitwise against `train.main()`, own-seed closest in 22/22); the plateau is defined in 19/19 runs with guard margins 40–160; the rules lineage `4c8508af… → 5ef6f731…` is accepted with the B2 sections identical; the gradient-probe sanity band (40–47 of 50 inside per run) clears the registered "≥ 5 on ≥ 3 runs"; every headline curve is on the run's full grid (51 points, nothing thinned).
- Verified: the 16 level-05 worlds are one seed each (seed 42); the reading is "across worlds", as the rules say. The May seeds are descriptive and, on rho at the encoder sites, disagree in direction (s44 rising).

## Allowed wording on the page

1. **Gain swing.** "The weights-only ceiling on how far the modulator could move each gain (the gain swing) grows throughout training, roughly linearly, so the point where it has made half of its rise comes after the survival plateau in all 16 worlds (the three May seeds agree). Five sites, one measure. The main network's own total weight norm, read with the same rule, is also 'late' in all 16 worlds, so this reading reflects how weight norms grow in this trainer, not a late awakening of the modulator." *(The control sentence requires the descriptive control curve, C2.)*
2. **Contextual fraction.** "The share of the modulator's gain signal that varies with the situation (rho) is lower at the end of training than at the untrained network, at the encoder and recurrent sites in all 16 worlds; half of that fall is complete by the first saved checkpoints, well before survival levels off ('early' in 15–16 of 16). At the actor and critic sites no consistent timing is found." *Not allowed:* "forms", "learns what to respond to", "context dependence appears".
3. **Freeze cost.** "Freezing the gain or the offset at its time-averaged value gives no timing reading: the cost's checkpoint-to-checkpoint wobble (11–28 survival steps) is as large as its net change over training, so the rule names no wake point in 15 of 16 worlds. This says nothing about the size of the cost. Descriptively, after the plateau the frozen agent survives fewer steps in every world (mean −33 steps for the gain, −31 for the offset, against roughly 250 live); no test of that difference was registered." *Not allowed:* "costs nothing", "no measurable effect".
4. **Update size, gradient probes.** "The modulator's relative step per checkpoint interval is roughly constant (≈ 0.10) across training; where its small net change clears the noise guard, its half-point is early. The share of the entropy-term gradient reaching the modulator rises from near zero at the untrained network to 8–37 % by the first saved checkpoint (early, 12 of 12 defined). The policy- and value-term shares from the single-batch probe are too noisy for the timing rule (no reading), as is the logged total share."
5. **Refutation, as registered.** "Under the registered rule, a measure reading 'early' refutes the explanation 'the modulated agent does not learn faster because the modulator wakes up late'; a measure reading 'late' supports it. Here the modulator's realised output (rho at three sites), its entropy-gradient share and its update size read 'early'; only the weights-only gain-swing ceiling reads 'late'. The rules do not combine measures." *Not allowed as a headline:* "consistent with 'wakes up late'".

## Cost of being wrong

No rerun and no data loss are at stake — every number needed is already on disk. Left as drafted, the page would carry three wrong sentences on a figure presented as evidence: one reversed in sign (rho "forms" when it falls), one an artefact any weight norm reproduces (swing "late" as "authority"), and one claiming no effect where every world shows a ~12 % survival cost (freeze). Together they would feed the "why doesn't the modulated agent learn faster" narrative with a story the same file contradicts, and that sentence is the kind that ends up in a paper.

## What flips the verdict

Text only: (1) rho sentence rewritten as a decline; (2) the swing gloss removed and the main-network-norm control shown beside it (CPU, minutes) — or, failing that, the swing reading stated as a weights-only ceiling with no "authority" claim; (3) the freeze sentence replaced by the "no timing reading + descriptive cost" form; (4) both directions of the registered refutation stated; (5) the page Summary `<dd>` corrected to the registered rule. With those, the reading table is **SUPPORTED WITH CAVEATS** (one seed per world; readings on positions 1 are "by the first checkpoint"; the sign-test null does not fit monotone measures).
