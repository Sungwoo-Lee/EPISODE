# The felt-injury modulator is a switch: small felt injury already means "hide"

## Key conclusion
When the modulator is fed only felt injury (the internal, lagging injury signal), its effect on behaviour saturates almost at once. Any felt injury above about 0.2 sends the agent into near-maximal bush hiding. This means the modulator cannot encode "how hurt", only "hurt or not". Combined with the healing confound, it explains why the modulator does not give a graded hypervigilance effect.

## Evidence, measurements, facts
- Clamp test on the felt-injury-input agent, 7M-step checkpoint: bush dwell is 18% at felt injury 0, 60% at 0.1, and 79-90% from 0.2.
- Felt injury starts at 0 each episode, lags body injury, and peaks at about 0.52 when body injury is 70.
