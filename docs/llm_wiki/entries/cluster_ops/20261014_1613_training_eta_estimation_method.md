# How to estimate when a training run will end

## Key conclusion
rPPO runs slow down as agents survive longer (more steps per episode), so the episode rate falls through training.
Use the run's own recent pace, measured from two tqdm progress readings about 20 min apart in its log, and allow for
further slowdown when it is below ~7M episodes. A queued run's duration comes from the seed-42 run of the same
world and agent on the same card class.

## Evidence, measurements, facts
- Initial guess "~5 h on Ada/4090, ~8 h on 2080 Ti" was wrong; half-factorial runs took 9-15 h on Ada/4090 and
  over 24 h on 2080 Ti.
- Late rate (7-10M) per world and agent, seed 42 on Ada/4090: 0.47-1.04 M episodes/h. 2080 Ti ≈ 0.6x; 3090 ≈ 1x
  for these small rPPO runs.
- Seed-43 runs finished 1-4 h faster than the seed-42 pace predicted (seed-to-seed variation, NAS load during seed 42).
- Working rule used: remaining = (10 − c)/r for c ≥ 6.5M; else early stretch at the mean of current and late rate,
  late rate ≈ current/(1.4 − 0.4·(c − 1)/6). Queue waits add ~25 min (two checks 15 min apart).

## Decisions and actions
- Report ETAs as ranges (± a couple of hours) and re-measure on request rather than reusing old guesses.
