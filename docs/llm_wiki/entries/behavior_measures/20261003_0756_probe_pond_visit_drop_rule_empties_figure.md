# Probe scenes in water worlds: truncate at first pond visit, don't drop cells

## Key conclusion
Revision 1 of the Basic Behaviour water plan dropped any probe scene x checkpoint cell where more than 3 of 30 episodes stepped onto the pond, to keep the probe about threat and cover. The plan-reviewer measured on pilot recordings that at the planned start hydration (100, pond 3 squares away) 59–62% of episodes reach the pond within 100 steps, so almost every trained cell would be dropped and the survivors selected by the very behaviour being measured. Revision 2 keeps every cell, measures bush time only up to the first pond step, reports the pond-reach share beside it, and chooses start hydration (150/165/180) by a rule fixed before calibration.

## Evidence, measurements, facts
- Pilot measurement (2 M-episode checkpoints): pond within 100 steps = 59–62% at start 90–110, 38% at 130–150, 15% at 150–170, 9–12% at 160–190.
- Calibration rule: lowest candidate with <=20% of episodes reaching the pond and <=2% over-drinking deaths, in both test worlds (10x10 and 20x20 whole-map smell).
- Re-check of Revision 2 found a collation bug: comparing against the sweep CSV at 1e-9 fails because `run_sweep.py` rounds to 4 decimals; tolerance 5e-5 adopted.

## Decisions and actions
- Revision 2 written into docs/develop/active/behavior/BASIC_BEHAVIOUR_WATER.md §R2 as defaults for the user to revisit; truncation lives in a new `probe_pond.py`, not in the shared sweep code.

## Open questions and follow-ups
