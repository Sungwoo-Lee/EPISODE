All 9 debates in debates.csv are drawn (100%), with 114 work-in-debate marks - a work counts once per debate however many positions it holds there. Spans are computed from positions.csv and checked against the first_year and latest_year columns of debates.csv; the build fails if they disagree. No debate in this corpus is settled; the direction of each `leaning` label is given in the status cards above the figure, because it runs to several lines. The statuses describe the 36 reviewed works, not the field.
